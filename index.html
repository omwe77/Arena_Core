METHODOLOGY

Tournament Simulation Methodology

ARENA_CORE generates tournament outcomes using a Poisson goal-distribution model parameterized by team attack/defense ratings and home advantage. The simulation runs deterministically from a seeded random number generator (Mulberry32 PRNG), so identical seeds reproduce identical brackets and match scores.

Tournament formats follow official competition structures: FIFA World Cup 48-team group + knockout, UEFA Champions League Swiss league + playoffs, and standard domestic league double round-robin. Results are generated client-side only, with no external data feeds.

ARENA_CORE does not provide betting advice, live predictions, or real-time stadium tracking. All historical standings and data represent packaged snapshots.
